Privacy

Updated 18 October 2026

Who is responsible

This is the personal academic website of Jermain Kaminski, Associate Professor of Entrepreneurship and Innovation at the School of Business and Economics, Maastricht University, Tongersestraat 53, 6211 LM Maastricht, The Netherlands. Jermain Kaminski is responsible for this website. For questions about privacy, email j.kaminski@maastrichtuniversity.nl.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub processes technical information such as your IP address, the page requested, the time of the request and your browser details, in order to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Website statistics

I use Plausible Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Plausible is hosted in the European Union, sets no cookies, stores no personal data and does not follow you across websites. Your IP address is only used, together with a salt that changes every day, to count unique visitors; it is never stored. I only see totals.

Cookies and other services

This website sets no cookies and uses no advertising or tracking tools other than the statistics described above, so it shows no cookie banner. Fonts are served from this website, not from Google Fonts. Some articles include YouTube videos. Nothing loads from YouTube until you click a video to play it; from then on, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and may store information in your browser under its own privacy policy. Links to other websites, such as Bluesky or journal pages, lead to services with their own privacy notices.

Email

If you email me, I receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. Enquiries about speaking, workshops and advisory work go to my private address, jermain.kaminski@gmail.com, which is handled by Google's Gmail service under Google's privacy policy. Email to a Maastricht University address is handled by the University's email service.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data about you by emailing j.kaminski@maastrichtuniversity.nl. The UM General Privacy Statement explains how Maastricht University handles personal data and how to reach its privacy team. You may also lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority.
