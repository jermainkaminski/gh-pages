My work brings together causal inference, language and vision to study how text, images and behaviour change financing and innovation outcomes.

I earned my PhD (summa cum laude) at RWTH Aachen in 2019 and was a visiting researcher with Thomas Malone and Peter Gloor at MIT’s Center for Collective Intelligence, and with César Hidalgo at the MIT Media Lab.

Before academia I worked in central marketing at Porsche, in executive consulting, and on data analytics in soccer for a Bundesliga club. My research with colleagues is cited by the OECD and the European Commission’s Joint Research Centre.
